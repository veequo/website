## Purpose and audience
Veqo is a newly launching, online-first service business. Its website markets guided student project delivery and end-to-end SaaS development, and converts visitors into enquiries or free-call bookings. Audiences are college students (B.E./B.Tech/MCA/BCA/M.Tech/M.Sc), mentors, founders, SMEs, and agencies.

Serve Bangalore, Chennai, Coimbatore, and beyond remotely. Do not imply a physical office or add maps, visiting instructions, or a public postal address. The original brief permits a registered-address placeholder only where legally required, flagged for review. Legal drafts are not validated legal advice or publishable policies.

## Brand and content
V = Vision; E = Enrichment; Q = Quality; O = Originality. Tone: confident, friendly, professional, youthful. Introduce values on Home, expand on About, emphasize learning/originality for students and discovery/quality for businesses. Avoid repetitive wording.

No fabricated statistics, clients, testimonials, outcomes, or team details. Mark editorial unknowns; hide unverified testimonial sections. Label illustrative portfolio concepts clearly. Student content must encourage participation, learning, attribution, and ethical use rather than buying work to submit as unaided.
